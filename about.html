Our Story

It all began with two cousins, a best friend, and summers spent in Grandma’s pantry. What started as playful adventures soon turned into a curiosity about the wonderful ingredients behind Grandma’s famous potato cornbread and her passion to create cakes whose aroma floats out the window and down the block. We watched, tasted, experimented, and dreamed of creating something of our own. That childhood curiosity grew into a passion for baking, leading us to culinary school and eventually bringing us full circle—back to Grandma’s old home. With love, determination, and a few treasured family recipes, we transformed her home into a warm, welcoming bakery. Twenty years later, we’re still baking, serving, and creating memories in the same spirit Grandma taught us. What began as three kids playing in a pantry has grown into a family tradition that continues to bring fresh-baked goodness and heartfelt hospitality to our community. From Grandma’s pantry to our family bakery—20 years of love, laughter, and serving our community.
